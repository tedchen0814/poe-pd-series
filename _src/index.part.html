PoE PD 互動教學 PoE PD 互動教學 Power over Ethernet · powered device, from RJ45 to DC-DC 4 × TOPICS

PoE PD（受電端設備）由兩段組成：前段的 PD 介面負責和 PSE 握手、安全地把電接進來；後段的 DC-DC 把 37–57 V 轉成板上電壓。四個互動頁分別說明架構、握手狀態機、功率預算，以及 DC-DC 拓撲的選擇，並整理 Layout、選料與除錯的注意事項。 A PoE powered device has two stages: the PD interface that handshakes with the PSE and brings power in safely, and a DC-DC converter that makes the board rails. Four interactive pages cover the architecture, the handshake, the power budget and the DC-DC topology choice.

怎麼使用 How to use

  1. 先看「01 架構總覽」建立整條電源鏈的概念。Start with the architecture page.
  2. 「02 握手狀態機」按播放或「下一段」，對照波形、狀態機與說明卡；切換故障情境看失敗時的樣子。Step through the handshake and try the fault scenarios.
  3. 「03 功率預算」選範例或自己調參數，看每一段損耗與規範檢查。Load a preset in the budget page and watch each loss.
  4. 「04 拓撲選擇」決定 DC-DC，再到 DC-DC 拓撲系列看 Flyback、ACF、Buck 的電路動畫。Pick a DC-DC topology, then open its animation in the DC-DC series.
